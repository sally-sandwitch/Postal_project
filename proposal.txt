Postal Project Proposal 

By: Adriana Jergusova, Spencer Ollmann, Alex Woods

Problem:
A post man on a college campus is trying to collect and deliver packages 
to and from each building in the shortest distance possible, with the 
carrying limit of 15Kg.

Graph:
On a separate file. 

Proposed approache:

Greedy using Dijkstra's algorithm since the data is a small enough and the collection of paths
is small enough that the time won't be affected too much making it faster to run.
Also theres enough path options that it will most likely choose the shortest path.

Package priority:

choose largest and highest priority package and any other smaller package
within weight limit and is on the path or going to the same destination.
